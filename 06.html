Eyes of a Blue Dog

motion conceptual experimental

A García Márquez adaptation. A black box dressed as a painter's studio. A story about dreams and forgetting.


Eyes of a Blue Dog is a short film adaptation of Gabriel García Márquez's story of the same name. A woman lives in a painter's studio. A man appears. They have met before, but only in dreams. She remembers him every time she wakes up. He forgets her every time he does. The phrase "eyes of a blue dog" was supposed to be the thing that made it stick.

The film was co-produced by E. Ayuso Rizzo, S. Antonova, and C. Woen. My contributions span art direction, editing, and sound design.

The studio was built from scratch inside a black box. No existing location, no found objects. Everything in the frame was placed there deliberately because the dialogue alone wasn't going to do it. The set had to communicate who this woman was, how long she had been there, and what kind of person lives like that. That's the design problem the project was built around.
