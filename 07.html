MARS

product graphic experimental research

A fully produced tabletop game about corporate extraction. Solo. From scratch.


MARS is a 2-6 player strategic board game set in a dystopian future where corporations compete to extract a rare resource from the red planet. The project was designed and produced individually during the final year of university at City University of Hong Kong, 2023. The deliverable was a fully playable, physically produced game: laser-cut acrylic tokens, printed cards, a game mat, a rulebook, and a complete component set.

The game was a vehicle for learning. Designing MARS meant working simultaneously across game design, graphic design, UX, and production, figuring out what each discipline required by running into the problems it presented. The mechanics determined the components. The components determined the graphic system. The graphic system had to hold together across a mat, 19 map tiles, 58 battle cards, 17 goal cards, 6 corporation sheets, and over 300 physical tokens.

The result is a functional game with a coherent visual identity. It has unresolved mechanical issues that were never addressed. Both of those things are true, and both are part of what the project was for.
